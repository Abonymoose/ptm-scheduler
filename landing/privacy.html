Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
PTM Now is a parent-teacher meeting scheduling tool used by schools. We take privacy seriously, especially because our service involves information about students and families. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we protect it.

Who we are

PTM Now ("we", "us") provides parent-teacher meeting scheduling software to schools. When a school uses PTM Now, the school is the owner of the data, and we process that data on the school's behalf.

Information we collect

We collect only what's needed to run meeting scheduling:

  • Account information — names and email addresses of teachers, parents, and school administrators.
  • Student information — a student's name and class section, used to label meetings. We do not collect grades, academic records, or other sensitive student data.
  • Booking information — which meetings have been booked, attendance, and any private notes a teacher records for their own reference.
  • Login codes — temporary six-digit codes sent by email to verify identity. These expire shortly after they are issued.

How we use information

We use the information solely to provide the service:

  • To let users sign in securely using an email code.
  • To show each person their relevant meeting schedule.
  • To let schools manage teachers, slots, and bookings.
  • To send transactional emails, such as your login code.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to operate PTM Now (for example, our email delivery and hosting providers).

How we protect information

Data is stored securely and transmitted over encrypted connections. Access is limited by role — parents see only their own family's bookings, and teachers see only their own meetings and notes. Private notes are visible only to their author.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as the school uses PTM Now. If a school stops using the service, its data can be deleted on request. Temporary login codes are deleted automatically after they expire.

Children's privacy

PTM Now is used by schools and by the parents of enrolled students. Student information is limited to a name and class section for the purpose of labelling meetings. Accounts are created by the school, not by children. If you believe a child's information has been handled improperly, contact us and we will address it promptly.

Your rights

Because the school owns the data, requests to access, correct, or delete personal information are handled through the school. Schools can contact us at any time to action these requests.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
