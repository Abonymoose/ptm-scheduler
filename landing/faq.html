Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know about PTM Now.
What is PTM Now?

PTM Now is a web app that takes the entire parent-teacher meeting process online — start to finish. It sweeps away the sign-up sheets and spreadsheets and replaces them with one system: parents book their slots in seconds, teachers see their whole day at a glance, and the school office runs everything from a single place, with complete transparency over every booking.

Who is it for?

PTM Now is built for schools of any size. Three groups use it together: the parents who book the meetings, the teachers who hold and manage them, and the office staff and administration who organise and oversee the entire day.

Do parents need to download an app?

No. PTM Now runs entirely in a web browser — on any phone, whether iOS or Android, any tablet, or any computer. Parents just open the link the school shares with them. There's nothing to install.

How do parents log in?

Parents enter their email and receive a six-digit code by email, which logs them straight in. It's secure, and there's no password to remember.

How does booking work?

Parents view the available slots for each teacher they need to see, add the ones they want to their cart, and confirm them all at once. There's also an auto-schedule option that automatically picks a set of non-conflicting slots across all their chosen teachers.

Can two parents book the same slot?

No. The system checks availability at the moment a booking is confirmed, so two parents can never end up in the same slot. Double-booking isn't just discouraged — it's completely impossible.

What if a parent has more than one child?

One login covers the entire family — every sibling included. Parents switch between their children with a single click, and can colour-code meetings by child to keep everything clear.

Can meetings be cancelled or changed?

Yes. Both parents and teachers can cancel a booking — which frees the slot for someone else — and book a different time. Teachers and administrators see these changes reflected live, and receive an email notification.

How do teachers use it?

Teachers set their slots and make them available to parents, then get a clear schedule of their whole meeting day. They can mark attendance for each meeting, keep private notes only they can see, and block off slots for breaks so parents can't book over them.

What can the school office do?

The school office and administrators manage teachers and their slots, set the meeting date, and can see exactly which parents haven't booked yet — so they can send reminders to just those families, instead of chasing everyone.

Is our data safe?

Yes. PTM Now stores only what it needs — students' names, classes, and sections — but never sensitive academic records. All access is role-based, so people see only their own information. Full details are in our privacy policy.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the school and how PTM Now is used. Get in touch and we'll talk through what works for you.

How does a school get started?

Getting set up takes very little time or effort. The school shares its teacher and class information, picks a meeting date, and the slots are generated automatically. Before committing, we arrange a pilot — a single PTM — to show exactly how it works.
