How it works

Simple for everyone involved.

PTM Now has one job: make parent-teacher meeting booking effortless — for the school office, for teachers, and for parents.

🏫 For the school

The office sets everything up once. Add your teachers, choose the meeting date, and slots are generated automatically for each teacher.

  • Add teachers with their name and subject
  • Set the PTM date — every slot updates to match
  • See at a glance which parents haven't booked yet
  • Manage any teacher's schedule from one place

👩‍🏫 For teachers

Teachers sign in with just their email and a code — no password to remember. Their whole meeting day is laid out clearly.

  • See every booked meeting with the student and parent
  • Block slots for breaks or lunch — parents can't book over them
  • Mark attendance for each meeting
  • Keep private notes on each meeting, searchable later

👪 For parents

Parents get a link and sign in with a six-digit code sent to their email. Booking takes seconds, from any phone.

  • One login for the whole family — switch between children with a tap
  • Pick slots into a cart and confirm them all at once
  • Or let auto-schedule find non-conflicting slots automatically
  • Nothing to install — it's just a website
